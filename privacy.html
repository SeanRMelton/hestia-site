HESTIA privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Who uses HESTIA

HESTIA is personal software. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by that owner. It has no other users and does not collect data from anyone else.

What Google data HESTIA accesses

  • Gmail: reads the owner's messages (read-only access) and creates drafts. HESTIA never sends email.
  • Google Calendar: reads the owner's events. Creates events on the owner's own calendar from the owner's own booking emails (tickets, appointments, travel bookings, bill due dates, deadlines and renewals), without inviting anyone, and tells the owner each time; any other event is created only after the owner explicitly confirms it. Removes an event only when the owner asks for it and confirms.
  • Google Tasks: reads the owner's reminders and tasks to show the ones due soon on HESTIA's own dashboard. Adds a reminder, marks one done, or removes one only when the owner asks for it (a removal only after the owner confirms).

How it is used

Only to provide features to the owner: answering the owner's own questions about their schedule, putting the owner's bookings and due dates on the owner's own calendar, keeping a private note of events the owner attended, preparing email drafts for the owner to review, and warning the owner about travel times and traffic for the owner's own appointments, and showing the owner's reminders that are due soon.

Where it is processed and stored

All Google data is processed on the owner's computer, by an AI model that runs locally. Google data, and anything derived from it, is never sent to any third-party service, including any third-party AI service. The one exception is travel times: the location of one of the owner's calendar events may be sent to Google's own Routes API (Google Maps Platform), together with the starting place and a departure time, to estimate how long the trip will take. Nothing else from the event, such as its title, description or attendees, is sent. Notes derived from it are stored in the owner's local files. Sign-in tokens are stored locally, readable only by the owner's account. An encrypted backup of the owner's local files is kept in the owner's own Google Drive. It is encrypted on the owner's machine before upload with a key Google never receives, so Google cannot read it.

Sharing

Google data is not sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising. No person other than the owner reads it. It is not used to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning model.

Google API Services User Data Policy

HESTIA's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Derived notes are kept in the owner's local files until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke HESTIA's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be sent to the support email shown on HESTIA's Google sign-in screen.
