HESTIA

HESTIA (Household Electronic Steward for Tasks, Information & Automation) is a personal home assistant. It runs on its owner's own computer and serves one household. It is not a public service, offers no accounts, and has no users other than its owner.

With the owner's permission it reads the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar so it can answer the owner's questions, note appointments, deliveries and bills, and prepare email drafts and calendar entries for the owner to approve.
